Academic Endeavors, Mission & Vision

I am an Assistant Professor and researcher whose academic foundation was shaped by systematic training in Mathematics, Systems Engineering, and Computer & Information Science across Egypt and leading United States institutions. My Ph.D. work investigated automated data integration, entity resolution, and knowledge discovery, bridging graph theory, machine learning, and natural language processing.

Information Science combines computer science, data science, library science, psychology, and cognitive ergonomics. Coming from an Electrical and Computer Engineering background, I was drawn to this interdisciplinary synthesis to understand how complex data architectures interact with real-world users and biomedical discovery pipelines.

Mission

To advance scientific discovery by fostering an accessible, ethical, and artificially intelligent data ecosystem. Driving automatic integration of scientific information, ensuring responsible and sustainable scientific computing, and empowering underrepresented groups in computational and data sciences.

Vision

A future where data quality, graph theory, and foundation models unite into transparent, ethical systems that accelerate biomedical discovery. To define the next generation of biomedical intelligence where structured relational networks clarify the underlying logic of life.
