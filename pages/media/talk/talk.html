Project 1: PubMed Knowledge Graph & MedGraph

Disambiguating and semantically querying millions of biomedical publications

The Challenge & Innovation

  • Biomedical Fragmentation: 35M+ PubMed papers lack unified semantic linkage between authors, clinical entities, and grants.
  • PubMed Knowledge Graph (PKG): Extracted entities using fine-tuned BERT; linked authors and NIH funding at massive scale.
  • MedGraph Architecture: Knowledge Graph Embeddings (TransE, RotatE) enabling conceptual search beyond simple keyword matching.

Project 2: Graph Neural Networks for Proteomics

Novel residue transition graphs for protein-protein interaction prediction (2025)

Direct Graph CNN Architecture

  • The Challenge: Experimental 3D protein structure determination is prohibitively slow; 1D sequence models miss spatial contact topology.
  • Our Method (2025): Developed an algorithm to infer Global Dense Residue Transition Graphs directly from primary amino acid sequences.
  • Directed GCNN: Captures directional residue constraints, achieving accurate protein-protein interaction (PPI) prediction without physical 3D coordinates.

Personal Frontier Research

Taming the AI Parameter Explosion

A Neuro-Symbolic & Graph-Based paradigm to bypass brute-force parameter scaling

The Critical AI Scaling Crisis: The Unsustainable Trillion-Parameter Trap

Modern foundation models achieve performance through massive brute-force overparameterization (billions to trillions of weights). This incurs extreme computational costs, massive energy footprints, and uncontrollable black-box hallucination.

My Independent Research Initiative

(Conducted as personal fundamental research alongside student lab projects)

  • Structured Relational Knowledge: Replace memorization of facts in dense weights with sparse, dynamically queried Graph Neural Networks (GNNs).
  • Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning: Ground neural representations in formal symbolic logic constraints to eliminate hallucination.
  • Drastic Parameter Compression: Target equivalent or superior reasoning fidelity with orders of magnitude fewer parameters.

Long-Term Strategic Impact

  • Green & Sustainable AI: Enables high-capability AI on edge and local compute clusters without requiring multi-million-dollar GPU farms.
  • Verifiable Reasoning: Logic-guided graph traversals guarantee interpretability and safety in high-stakes domains.
  • Intellectual Leadership for Egypt: Establishes a unique, high-impact theoretical and algorithmic contribution originating from Nile University.
[Min 11-13] Present this personal frontier project: - Clarify that while my students work on translational biomedical AI (segmentation, proteomics, data engineering), this is my personal core passion project. - Modern AI is trapped in brute-force scaling: more parameters, more GPUs, more power. - My solution leverages Neuro-Symbolic AI and GNNs: structured knowledge graphs can represent concepts and relations symbolically, drastically shrinking parameter size while enhancing reasoning fidelity. - This positions Nile University at the forefront of post-scaling AI architectures.
