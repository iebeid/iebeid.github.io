Fatherhood & Family Life

Devoted fatherhood, mentorship of youth, moral stewardship, and family bonds.

Core Life Priority

Above all academic titles, research accomplishments, and professional endeavors, my most treasured and defining role is being a devoted father. Nurturing, guiding, and supporting my children stands at the very center of my life and informs the ethical perspective I bring to scholarship, education, and community stewardship.

Fatherhood is an active daily practice of patience, unconditional love, presence, and intellectual curiosity. Whether exploring nature on weekend hikes, discovering science and history together, discussing ethics, or encouraging creative expression, sharing these moments of wonder with my family is my greatest source of joy, purpose, and balance.
