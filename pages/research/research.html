ProtGram-DirectGCN

Frontiers 2025

A novel method for protein-protein interaction prediction using directed graph convolutional neural networks. This work infers global dense residue transition graphs directly from primary amino acid sequences, enabling accurate interaction screening without requiring physical 3D crystallographic coordinates.

Paper (Front. Bioinform.)

VisInt-X & XLPM: Cross-Linked Proteomics Suite

Electronic Imaging / JIST

An end-to-end chemical cross-linking mass spectrometry (XL-MS) curation and visual analytics platform. Raw Orbitrap MS/MS spectra undergo heavy relational curation (15-table xlpmwebv1 schema & Perl engine with 5 ppm precursor / 0.8 Da b/y fragment matching, 14N/15N isotopic filtering, and C-Score ranking) and are explored through a 3-tier D3.js matrix/treemap synchronized with a 3Dmol.js WebGL dual-protein viewer.

TransER: Unsupervised Entity Resolution

KDD Draft

Transformational Embeddings for Data Quality

TransER introduces an unsupervised entity resolution approach via transformational embeddings, mitigating the quadratic complexity of traditional pairwise comparisons. It leverages graph neural network concepts and transformational embeddings analogous to TransE to automate data cleaning, curation, and integration without handcrafting syntactic rules.

Entity Resolution Unsupervised Learning Data Curation

MedGCN: Biomedical NER

MCBIOS 2024

Query-time Character Level Embedding via Bidirectional GCNs

A novel approach to Biomedical Named Entity Recognition (NER) that recasts the problem as a character-level representation of tokens in a directed graph. Trained via a Bidirectional Graph Convolutional Neural Network, MedGCN operates on the fly without the overhead or carbon footprint of fine-tuning large language models.

Biomedical NER Graph Convolutional Networks NLP
