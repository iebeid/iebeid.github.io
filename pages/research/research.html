Current & Recent

ProtGram-DirectGCN

Frontiers 2025

A novel method for protein-protein interaction prediction using directed graph convolutional neural networks. This work infers global dense residue transition graphs directly from primary amino acid sequences, enabling accurate interaction screening without requiring physical 3D crystallographic coordinates.

Paper (Front. Bioinform.)

Multimodal Medical Segmentation

NSF Award #2523786

Collaborative Research: CISE-MSI project ($600K total / $210K Lead PI) developing auto-prompt textual annotations with domain knowledge for medical image segmentation foundation models. Introduces Spatially Localized Channel Attention (SLCA) validated across clinical imaging benchmarks.

NSF CISE/CCF • IEEE CBMS 2025

VisInt-X & XLPM: Cross-Linked Proteomics Suite

Electronic Imaging / JIST

An end-to-end chemical cross-linking mass spectrometry (XL-MS) curation and visual analytics platform. Raw Orbitrap MS/MS spectra undergo heavy relational curation (15-table xlpmwebv1 schema & Perl engine with 5 ppm precursor / 0.8 Da b/y fragment matching, 14N/15N isotopic filtering, and C-Score ranking) and are explored through a 3-tier D3.js matrix/treemap synchronized with a 3Dmol.js WebGL dual-protein viewer.

PubMed Knowledge Graph & MedGraph

Nature Sci Data

Construction of a comprehensive PubMed Knowledge Graph with fine-tuned BERT entity extraction, author disambiguation, and NIH grant linkage. MedGraph uses graph representation embeddings (TransE, RotatE) for semantic biomedical search.

ModER: Unsupervised Entity Resolution

Graph ER

Graph-based unsupervised entity resolution using Composite Modularity Optimization and Locality-Sensitive Hashing (LSH) for resolving ambiguous records in large-scale knowledge graphs.
