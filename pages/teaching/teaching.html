Teaching Philosophy & Methodology

My approach to education is rooted in a deep-seated familial legacy of educators. Witnessing my mother’s dedication as an elementary teacher and the stories of my grandfather’s classroom mastery instilled in me the conviction that teaching is more than the transfer of knowledge; it is the building of confidence in high-stakes, rigorous computational disciplines.

Live Coding & Debugging

Demonstrating real-time debugging to prove compiler errors are diagnostic learning moments rather than failures.

Research-Teaching Synergy

Using authentic open-source datasets and biomedical knowledge graph challenges instead of sterile toy exercises.

Guild-Based Mentorship

Pair programming, peer code reviews, and HyFlex accessibility for local and remote working students.
